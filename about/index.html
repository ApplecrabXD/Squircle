What's this Squircle?

A square tis but a simple item.

Strong and solid. and the most default shape A bit stiff, though.

What about the opposite. A circle.

Smooth and friendly, still a default shape and bit hard to stack.

What's the middle?

Welcome To squircle: a strong square yet a friendly circle. It's the shape of everything we make, and how we like to work. A bit of both.

It started with a drawer.

  1. 01

    2021

    The drawer of dead gadgets.

    You know the one. A phone with a tired battery, a laptop missing one key, a watch that just stopped. All glued shut, and all cheaper to bin than to fix.

  2. 02

    2022

    One rule: it has to open.

    So we gave ourselves a rule. If we make it, you can open it. No glue, no rivets, no special tools. One screwdriver, and every part comes out.

  3. 03

    2023

    Lappy, on a kitchen table.

    The first Lappy came together on a kitchen table Swappable ports, upgradeable RAM and a back panel that lifts right off. It worked, and people wanted one.

  4. 04

    2026

    Six products later.

    Now there's a gaming PC, a handheld, a folding phone, a watch… and a microwave. Yes, really. Different shapes, same rule: if it breaks, you can fix it.

Four blocks, four rules.

Our logo is four blocks stacked inside each other. Each one stands for something, and together they make Squircle.

  1. The orange square

    Open by design.

    Everything we make comes apart with one screwdriver. No glue, no rivets, no secrets. If you can't open it, you don't really own it.

  2. The grey circle

    Built to last.

    Good parts, spares for years and upgrades whenever you want them. The greenest gadget is the one you never have to replace.

  3. The purple square

    Your build, your call.

    Grab a DIY kit and fit every part yourself, or let us build it for you. Either way, you pick what goes inside.

  4. The white circle

    Have some fun.

    Swappable panels, a microwave you can fix and a secret or two hidden around this site. Tech should make you smile.

  5. Stack them up.

    Four blocks. One Squircle.

Squircle isn't a real company. Yet. :(

It's a Web Dev final project, designed and built by ApplecrabXD. Every page is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks, databases or lookup tables, (yes that was a stupid idea, do as I say not as I do) With GSAP doing the scroll magic. Nothing here is actually for sale, but every line of code is real.
